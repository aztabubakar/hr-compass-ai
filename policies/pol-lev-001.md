# Leave of Absence Policy

**Document ID:** POL-LEV-001  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Version:** 1.4  
**Owner:** People & Culture Team

Covers parental, medical/FMLA, bereavement, jury duty, military, and personal unpaid leave, including documentation and job-protection rules.

## Purpose and Scope

This policy describes the types of leave of absence available to eligible Solara Dynamics employees beyond standard PTO, including parental, medical, bereavement, jury duty, military, and personal leave, and explains how each interacts with job protection under applicable law.

## Parental Leave

Eligible employees receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave for the birth, adoption, or fostering of a child, regardless of gender or parent role. Birth parents are additionally eligible for up to 6 to 8 weeks of paid medical recovery leave through short-term disability, for a combined total of up to approximately 18 to 20 weeks. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event and may be taken continuously or, with manager approval, in blocks of no less than 2 weeks, for example to ease a return to work.

## Medical Leave and FMLA Coordination

Employees with a serious health condition may take medical leave supported by a healthcare provider's certification submitted to the People & Culture Team. For employees who meet federal Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) eligibility requirements (12 months of employment, 1,250 hours worked in the prior 12 months, and employment at a location with 50 or more employees within 75 miles), medical leave runs concurrently with FMLA leave, providing up to 12 weeks of job-protected leave per rolling 12-month period.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees receive up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, partner, child, parent, or sibling) and up to 2 paid days for the death of an extended family member. Additional unpaid bereavement leave may be approved by the manager on a case-by-case basis.

## Jury Duty and Civic Leave

Solara Dynamics provides full pay for the duration of jury duty service, with no limit on the number of days, upon presentation of a jury summons to the People & Culture Team. Employees called as a witness in a legal proceeding unrelated to Solara Dynamics business receive unpaid civic leave with job protection.

## Military Leave

Employees who are members of the uniformed services are granted leave and reemployment rights in accordance with the Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act (USERRA) and applicable state law, including continued benefits coverage options during leave as required by law.

## Personal Leave of Absence (Unpaid)

Employees who have exhausted PTO and do not qualify for another leave category may request an unpaid personal leave of absence of up to 30 calendar days, subject to VP approval. Extensions beyond 30 days are evaluated on a case-by-case basis and are not guaranteed to preserve the employee's position, though the company will make reasonable efforts to identify a suitable returning role where possible.

## Requesting a Leave and Required Documentation

All leave requests are submitted through Compass HR under 'Leave of Absence' at least 30 calendar days in advance when the need for leave is foreseeable (such as parental leave), or as soon as practicable for unforeseeable leave (such as a medical emergency). Medical certifications must be submitted to the People & Culture Team, not to the employee's manager, to protect confidentiality; managers are informed only that leave has been approved, not the underlying medical details.

## Job Protection and Return to Work

Employees on FMLA-qualifying medical leave or approved parental leave are reinstated to the same or an equivalent position upon return, consistent with applicable law. Employees on personal leave of absence are not guaranteed reinstatement but will be considered for open, suitable positions if their prior role is no longer available at the time they are ready to return.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use PTO to top up my pay during unpaid personal leave? No, personal leave of absence under Section 7 is unpaid by design; if you have unused PTO, you should use it before your leave begins rather than during it.

Do I need to tell my manager the medical reason for my leave? No, medical certifications go to the People & Culture Team only; your manager is informed that leave was approved, not the underlying medical details, as described in Section 8.

Can I take parental leave if I am adopting rather than giving birth? Yes, the 12-week paid parental leave in Section 2 applies equally to birth, adoption, and foster placement; only the additional medical recovery leave is specific to birth parents.

## Roles and Responsibilities

The People & Culture Team owns leave administration, collects and safeguards medical certifications, and determines FMLA eligibility for each request. Employees are responsible for submitting leave requests with as much advance notice as possible and for providing required documentation promptly.

Managers are responsible for supporting a smooth handoff of responsibilities before a leave begins and for welcoming the employee back into their same or an equivalent role upon return, without requesting medical details the employee has not chosen to share.
