# Remote Work Policy

**Document ID:** POL-RWK-001  
**Effective Date:** 2024-03-01  
**Version:** 1.3  
**Owner:** People & Culture Team

Defines Solara Dynamics' onsite, hybrid, and remote work categories, collaboration expectations, and the rules for temporary 'Work From Anywhere' remote work.

## Purpose and Philosophy

Solara Dynamics, Inc. supports a flexible, outcomes-oriented work environment because we believe employees do their best work when they have control over where and, within reason, when they work. This policy defines the work arrangement categories available to employees and the expectations that come with each. It works together with the Work Location, Interstate & International Work Policy (POL-LOC-001), which governs where an employee may legally and administratively perform work, and the Information Security & Data Protection Policy (POL-SEC-001), which governs how employees must connect to company systems while working outside an office.

## Work Arrangement Categories

Every employee is assigned one of three work arrangement categories in Compass HR, recorded on their employee profile alongside their designated work location:

- Onsite: works from an assigned Solara Dynamics office at least 4 days per week. This is typical for roles requiring hands-on lab access or frequent in-person customer visits.
- Hybrid: works from an assigned Solara Dynamics office at least 2 days per week; the default arrangement for employees who live within commuting distance of the Austin, Denver, or Raleigh offices.
- Remote: has no regular office attendance expectation and works from an approved home work location, whether or not that location is near a Solara Dynamics office.

## Eligibility Criteria

Remote and hybrid eligibility is determined by role, not by tenure or performance rating. Roles that require regular access to physical lab equipment, in-person customer demonstrations, or office-based administrative duties (such as Facilities or certain IT hardware roles) are designated Onsite or Hybrid in the job requisition and offer letter. All other roles default to Remote-eligible unless the hiring manager documents a specific business reason otherwise and that reason is reviewed by the People & Culture Team before the requisition is posted.

An employee's work arrangement category may change over time if their role changes (for example, moving from an individual contributor engineering role into a Facilities-coordination role), but such a change is treated as a formal work arrangement change under Section 8, not an automatic side effect of a role change.

## Home Office Requirements and Stipend

Remote and hybrid employees must maintain a dedicated, private workspace with a reliable internet connection of at least 25 Mbps download / 5 Mbps upload, and must connect to internal systems only through SolaraSecure VPN per the Information Security & Data Protection Policy (POL-SEC-001). A dedicated workspace does not need to be a separate room, but it should be a consistent location that supports confidential video calls and secure handling of any company equipment.

New remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time $150 home office setup stipend, separate from standard equipment issued under the Company Equipment & Asset Policy (POL-EQP-001), which can be used for items such as a desk, chair, or lighting. The stipend is requested through SolaraExpense within the first 60 days of employment or of transitioning to a remote or hybrid arrangement.

## Core Collaboration Hours

All employees, regardless of work arrangement, must be reachable and available for meetings between 10:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m. in their assigned local business time zone (Eastern, Central, Mountain, or Pacific). Outside of core hours, employees may structure their schedule flexibly to meet their role's responsibilities, provided they remain responsive to reasonable requests from teammates in other time zones within a business day.

Teams that span multiple time zones are encouraged to rotate meeting times so that the same team members are not always the ones asked to join outside their preferred working hours.

## Temporary Remote Work ('Work From Anywhere')

Employees may temporarily work from a location other than their designated work location under the 'Work From Anywhere' allowance, which is intended to support things like visiting family, a change of scenery, or working from a vacation home for a limited time, subject to the following limits:

- Up to 20 business days (approximately 4 weeks) per calendar year may be used without special approval, provided the destination is a U.S. state where Solara Dynamics is already payroll-registered (see POL-LOC-001) and within the continental U.S. time zones.
- Requests exceeding 20 business days in a calendar year, requests to work from a U.S. state not yet payroll-registered, or any international destination, are not covered by this allowance and must instead go through the Work Location Change Request process defined in POL-LOC-001, regardless of the requested duration. This applies even when the total time requested, such as six consecutive weeks, might otherwise seem like a simple extension of the allowance.
- Manager notice (via Compass HR) is required even for allowance-eligible temporary remote work of any length, so that the team calendar and any on-call schedules stay accurate.

## Performance Expectations

Work arrangement does not change performance expectations. Remote and hybrid employees are evaluated on the same goals, quality standards, and collaboration expectations as onsite employees, using the same performance review cycle and rubric. Managers may require temporary return-to-office arrangements for specific business needs, such as an important customer visit or team offsite, with at least 2 weeks' notice except in urgent circumstances.

## Requesting a Change in Work Arrangement

Employees who wish to permanently change their work arrangement category (for example, from Hybrid to Remote, or from Remote to Hybrid) must submit a request through Compass HR with manager and People & Culture approval. Approval considers role requirements, team collaboration needs, and, if the request involves a change of state or country, compliance with the Work Location, Interstate & International Work Policy (POL-LOC-001). Decisions are typically communicated within 10 business days of submission.

## Frequently Asked Questions

I want to work from a different state for six weeks - does the 'Work From Anywhere' allowance cover that? Not automatically. Six weeks is roughly 30 business days, which exceeds the 20 business day no-approval allowance in Section 7, so the request must go through the Work Location Change Request process in POL-LOC-001, even if the destination state is one where Solara Dynamics is already payroll-registered.

I want to work from another country for six weeks - is that covered by this policy? International work of any length is never covered by the 'Work From Anywhere' allowance in this policy; it always requires review under POL-LOC-001, including confirmation that the destination country is on the approved Employer of Record list.

Do I need approval to work from a coffee shop across town for a day? No, working from a different location within the same metro area and state does not change your designated work location or trigger any approval process; this policy is concerned with work location changes that cross state or country lines or that last for an extended period.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for notifying their manager of any temporary remote work location and for submitting a Work Location Change Request in Compass HR when a planned absence exceeds the 'Work From Anywhere' allowance or involves a new state or country, well before the relevant lead time in POL-LOC-001. Managers are responsible for confirming team coverage and collaboration expectations are still met during any temporary remote work period.

The People & Culture Team maintains the eligibility criteria for each work arrangement category and reviews permanent work arrangement change requests. IT Security ensures that SolaraSecure VPN access and MDM enrollment remain in place for any employee working temporarily from a new location.
