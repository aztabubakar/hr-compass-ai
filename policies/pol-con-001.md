# Workplace Conduct & Anti-Harassment Policy

**Document ID:** POL-CON-001  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Version:** 1.7  
**Owner:** People & Culture Team

Sets standards of conduct, equal employment opportunity, anti-harassment/anti-discrimination rules, reporting, and non-retaliation protections.

## Purpose and Scope

Solara Dynamics, Inc. is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and safe workplace for every employee, contractor, and visitor. This policy applies to conduct in the workplace, at company events, and in virtual or remote work settings, including messages sent through company chat and video conferencing tools.

## Standards of Conduct

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with professionalism and respect, to act with honesty and integrity, and to comply with all applicable laws and company policies in the course of their work, both inside and outside normal business hours when representing the company.

## Equal Employment Opportunity

Solara Dynamics is an equal opportunity employer and makes employment decisions based on qualifications, merit, and business need, without regard to race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law. This principle applies to hiring, promotion, compensation, and every other aspect of employment.

## Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination

Harassment or discrimination based on any protected characteristic is strictly prohibited, whether committed by a manager, coworker, customer, or vendor, and regardless of whether it occurs in person, in writing, or through digital communication tools. Prohibited conduct includes unwelcome sexual advances, derogatory comments or jokes, and creation of a hostile work environment, and applies equally at in-person offices, virtual meetings, and offsite company events.

## Reporting Procedures

Employees who experience or witness conduct that may violate this policy should report it to their manager, any People & Culture Team member, or through the confidential Solara Ethics Hotline, which accepts anonymous reports and is operated by an independent third party. Reports may be made verbally or in writing, and employees are never required to confront the individual involved directly before reporting.

## Investigation Process

The People & Culture Team acknowledges all reports within 5 business days and conducts a prompt, impartial investigation. Investigations are kept as confidential as possible, consistent with the need to conduct a thorough review and take appropriate corrective action, and both the reporting employee and the subject of the report are informed of the general outcome once the investigation concludes.

## Non-Retaliation

Solara Dynamics strictly prohibits retaliation against any employee who reports a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation under this policy. Employees who believe they have experienced retaliation should report it immediately using the channels described in Section 5, and retaliation claims are treated with the same seriousness as the original report.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest, including outside employment, financial interests in competitors or vendors, and close personal relationships with individuals they supervise, using the annual Conflict of Interest disclosure in Compass HR, and should also disclose new conflicts as they arise rather than waiting for the annual cycle.

## Disciplinary Action

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, regardless of the violator's position or tenure. The severity of disciplinary action considers the nature and impact of the violation and any prior conduct history, and is determined in consultation with the People & Culture Team and, where appropriate, Legal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can I report a concern anonymously? Yes, the Solara Ethics Hotline described in Section 5 accepts anonymous reports through an independent third party; you do not have to give your name to have a concern investigated.

What if the person I need to report is my own manager? Report it to any People & Culture Team member or through the Solara Ethics Hotline instead of your manager; you are never required to report a concern to the person the concern is about.

Will I be punished for reporting something that turns out not to be a violation? No, good-faith reports are protected under the non-retaliation provisions in Section 6 even if the investigation does not substantiate a policy violation.

## Roles and Responsibilities

The People & Culture Team owns this policy, operates the reporting channels described in Section 5, and leads or coordinates investigations. Every employee, manager, and contractor is responsible for treating others with respect and for reporting suspected violations rather than ignoring them.

Managers have an additional responsibility to model respectful conduct, to escalate any concern raised by a direct report to the People & Culture Team promptly, and to never attempt to personally investigate or resolve a harassment complaint themselves.
