Business Expense & Reimbursement Policy

Covers approved expense categories, per diem rates, submission deadlines, documentation requirements, and approval thresholds.

Purpose

This policy defines which business expenses Solara Dynamics reimburses, how to submit them, and the approvals required, so employees can be reimbursed quickly and Finance can maintain accurate, auditable records of company spending.

Approved Expense Categories

Reimbursable expense categories include business travel (see the Business Travel Policy, POL-TRV-001), client meals and entertainment, approved software and subscriptions under $50/month not already provided by IT, professional development (conferences, courses, certifications) up to $2,000 per employee per year, and coworking space day-passes for remote employees traveling for work who need a professional space for client calls.

Expenses outside these categories may still be reimbursed with advance written approval from the employee's manager and Finance, provided there is a clear, documented business purpose.

Per Diem Rates for Travel Meals

Employees traveling for business may either submit itemized meal receipts or claim a flat per diem instead: $75 per full travel day for domestic travel, and $100 per full travel day for international travel. Per diem amounts are prorated to $25 for partial travel days (departure or return day). Employees should choose one method (itemized or per diem) for an entire trip rather than mixing methods within the same trip.

Submission Process and Deadlines

All expenses must be submitted through the SolaraExpense tool within 30 calendar days of the expense being incurred. Expenses submitted more than 60 days after the expense date will not be reimbursed except in cases of documented extenuating circumstances approved by Finance, such as an extended medical leave that delayed submission.

Receipts and Documentation Requirements

Itemized receipts are required for any single expense over $25. For expenses claimed under the meal per diem, no receipt is required. All expense submissions must include a brief business purpose and, for client-facing expenses, the names of attendees and their company affiliation, so that Finance can properly categorize the expense for accounting and tax purposes.

Corporate Card vs. Reimbursement

Employees who travel for business more than once per quarter, or whose role regularly incurs client entertainment expenses, are issued a Solara Dynamics corporate card. Corporate card transactions still require documentation in SolaraExpense within 30 days but do not require the employee to pay out of pocket. All other employees are reimbursed for approved out-of-pocket expenses via payroll within one pay cycle of approval, typically within 10 business days.

Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Solara Dynamics does not reimburse personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, personal travel upgrades beyond the class defined in the Business Travel Policy (POL-TRV-001), alcohol at internal (non-client) events without VP approval, or expenses lacking a clear business purpose. Expense reports that include non-reimbursable items will have those specific line items rejected rather than the entire report being denied.

International Expenses and Currency Conversion

Expenses incurred in a foreign currency are converted to U.S. dollars using the exchange rate on the transaction date as reported by the corporate card issuer, or, for cash reimbursements, the rate published by SolaraExpense on the date of submission. Employees should retain the original-currency receipt in addition to the converted amount shown in SolaraExpense.

Approval Thresholds

Expense reports up to $1,000 require direct manager approval. Expense reports between $1,000 and $5,000 require VP approval in addition to manager approval. Expense reports over $5,000 additionally require Finance approval before reimbursement is processed. These thresholds apply to the total value of a single expense report, not to each individual line item within it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I expense a meal for a candidate I am interviewing? Yes, candidate meals are treated the same as client meals under Section 2 and should include the candidate's name and the role they are interviewing for as the business purpose.

What if I lose a receipt for an expense over $25? Contact the vendor for a duplicate receipt if possible; if that is not possible, submit the expense with a written explanation and your manager may still approve it at their discretion, though this should not become a regular practice.

Can I be reimbursed in a currency other than U.S. dollars? No, all reimbursements are paid in U.S. dollars through payroll using the conversion approach in Section 8, regardless of the currency the original expense was incurred in.

Roles and Responsibilities

The Finance Team owns this policy, sets per diem rates and approval thresholds, and audits expense reports for compliance. Employees are responsible for submitting accurate, timely expense reports with appropriate documentation and for understanding which categories are reimbursable before incurring an expense.

Managers are the first approver for their direct reports' expense reports and are responsible for confirming a clear business purpose before approving. VPs and Finance apply additional review at the higher approval thresholds described in Section 9.
