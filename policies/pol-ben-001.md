# Employee Benefits Policy

**Document ID:** POL-BEN-001  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Version:** 1.5  
**Owner:** People & Culture Team

Summarizes medical/dental/vision plans, 401(k) matching, FSA, insurance, and benefits eligibility rules by employment type.

## Purpose and Eligibility

Solara Dynamics, Inc. offers a competitive benefits package to support employee health and financial wellbeing. Full-time employees (scheduled 30 or more hours per week) are eligible for medical, dental, and vision coverage beginning the first of the month following 30 days of continuous employment. Part-time employees scheduled under 30 hours per week are not eligible for medical, dental, or vision coverage but are eligible for the 401(k) plan described in Section 4.

Independent contractors and EOR-employed international team members are not eligible for benefits under this policy and instead receive statutory benefits, if any, through their contractor agreement or local EOR, which may include benefits such as national health coverage that are not applicable to U.S.-based direct employees.

## Health, Dental, and Vision Plans

Eligible employees may choose from three medical plan options: Solara Bronze PPO, Solara Gold PPO, and Solara HDHP+HSA. The Bronze PPO has the lowest monthly premium with higher out-of-pocket costs; the Gold PPO has a higher premium with lower out-of-pocket costs; the HDHP+HSA plan pairs a high-deductible health plan with a Health Savings Account that Solara Dynamics contributes to annually. Dental and vision coverage are offered as separate elections and can be combined with any medical plan choice.

Solara Dynamics pays 80% of the employee-only premium for all three medical plans and 60% of the premium for enrolled dependents (spouse, domestic partner, and/or children). Detailed plan summaries, including deductibles and out-of-pocket maximums, are available in Compass HR under 'Benefits > Plan Documents.'

## Waiting Period

New employees must complete a 30-calendar-day waiting period before medical, dental, and vision coverage begins. Coverage becomes effective on the first day of the month following the waiting period; for example, an employee starting on March 10 completes the waiting period on April 9 and has coverage effective May 1. Employees must complete benefits enrollment in Compass HR within 14 days of their hire date or wait until the next open enrollment period, unless they experience a qualifying life event as described in Section 9.

## 401(k) Retirement Plan and Match

All employees, including part-time employees, are eligible to enroll in the 401(k) plan after 90 days of employment. Solara Dynamics matches 100% of employee contributions on the first 3% of eligible compensation and 50% of the next 2%, for a maximum employer match of 4% of eligible compensation when an employee contributes at least 5%. All employer matching contributions vest immediately, meaning employees keep 100% of the match even if they leave shortly after receiving it.

## Flexible Spending Accounts

Eligible employees may enroll in a Healthcare Flexible Spending Account (FSA) and/or a Dependent Care FSA during open enrollment or within 30 days of a qualifying life event. FSA contribution limits follow the annual IRS limits published each year in Compass HR, and unused Healthcare FSA funds up to the IRS-allowed carryover amount roll over to the next plan year.

## Life and Disability Insurance

Solara Dynamics provides basic life insurance and short-term disability insurance at no cost to all benefits-eligible employees, at a coverage amount of one times annual base salary for life insurance. Employees may purchase supplemental life insurance for themselves and dependents at group rates during enrollment, and long-term disability coverage is also available as a voluntary, employee-paid benefit.

## Wellness Stipend

Benefits-eligible employees receive a $50 per month wellness stipend, reimbursable through SolaraExpense, for expenses such as gym memberships, fitness classes, or mental health app subscriptions. Unused monthly stipend amounts do not roll over to future months.

## Benefits for Part-Time and Contract Workers

As described in Section 1, part-time employees are eligible only for the 401(k) plan and are not eligible for medical, dental, vision, life, or disability insurance, or the wellness stipend. Contractors and EOR-employed international staff are not eligible for any benefit described in this policy, regardless of how many hours they work.

## Qualifying Life Events and Open Enrollment

Open enrollment occurs each year in November for coverage effective January 1. Outside of open enrollment, employees may change benefits elections within 30 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage, divorce, birth or adoption of a child, or loss of other coverage, by submitting documentation in Compass HR. Changes made outside a qualifying life event or open enrollment window are not permitted under IRS Section 125 rules governing pre-tax benefits elections.

## Frequently Asked Questions

I am a part-time employee - can I buy into the medical plan at full cost? No, medical, dental, and vision plans are only available to full-time employees under this policy; part-time employees cannot enroll even if willing to pay the full premium themselves.

When does my 401(k) match start? The employer match begins as soon as you enroll and start contributing, which can happen any time after your 90-day eligibility date; there is no separate waiting period for the match itself once you are contributing.

I just got married - how long do I have to add my spouse to my plan? You have 30 days from the date of marriage to submit the qualifying life event and required documentation in Compass HR; after 30 days, you must wait until the next open enrollment.

## Roles and Responsibilities

The People & Culture Team owns benefits plan selection, runs annual open enrollment, and is the point of contact for eligibility questions and qualifying life event documentation. Payroll administers premium deductions, FSA contributions, and 401(k) contribution processing each pay period.

Employees are responsible for completing enrollment within the required windows, keeping dependent information current, and reviewing plan documents before selecting a medical plan option. Managers have no role in benefits elections and should direct any benefits questions from their team to the People & Culture Team.
