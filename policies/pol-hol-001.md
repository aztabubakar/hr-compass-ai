# Company Holiday Schedule Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HOL-001  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Version:** 1.4  
**Owner:** People & Culture Team

Lists Solara Dynamics' observed U.S. holidays, floating holidays, the winter office closure, and holiday pay rules.

## Purpose

This policy defines the paid holidays observed by Solara Dynamics, Inc. for U.S.-based employees each calendar year and explains how holidays are handled for non-U.S. team members, non-exempt staff, and employees whose PTO overlaps with a holiday. It is designed to give every employee a predictable annual holiday calendar they can plan around well in advance.

## Observed U.S. Holidays

Solara Dynamics observes the following 10 paid holidays each year for all U.S.-based employees, regardless of work location, work arrangement, or employment type (full-time or part-time):

- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Memorial Day
- Juneteenth
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve
- Christmas Day

## Floating Holidays

In addition to the 10 fixed holidays, each U.S.-based employee receives 2 floating holidays per calendar year to use for holidays of personal, cultural, or religious significance not on the standard list above, such as Diwali, Lunar New Year, Eid, or Yom Kippur. Floating holidays are prorated for employees hired after July 1 of a given year (one floating holiday instead of two) and do not carry over to the following year; unused floating holidays are forfeited on December 31.

Floating holidays are requested through Compass HR in the same way as PTO under the Paid Time Off Policy (POL-PTO-001), and the same manager-approval requirement applies, although floating holidays are tracked as a separate balance from standard PTO so that using one does not reduce an employee's regular PTO balance.

## Holidays for Non-U.S. Employees and Contractors

Employees engaged through an Employer of Record outside the United States observe the statutory public holiday schedule of their country of employment, as documented in their local EOR employment agreement, rather than the U.S. holiday list in Section 2. This means, for example, that an employee working through Solara Dynamics' EOR arrangement in the United Kingdom observes UK bank holidays such as the Early May Bank Holiday instead of the U.S. Independence Day holiday. Independent contractors do not receive paid holidays under this policy, consistent with their engagement as non-employees.

## Winter Office Closure

Solara Dynamics offices are closed from December 26 through December 31 each year ('Winter Recess'). These days are paid company closure days for all U.S.-based employees and are not deducted from PTO balances or floating holiday balances. Winter Recess is intended to give the whole company a shared period of rest at year-end, and employees are discouraged from scheduling meetings or expecting responses to non-urgent messages during this window.

Non-exempt employees who are required to work during Winter Recess to support customer operations (for example, a small on-call customer support rotation) receive holiday premium pay as described in Section 6, and are offered the choice of an alternate day off within the following 60 days in addition to the premium pay.

## Holiday Pay for Non-Exempt Employees

Non-exempt (hourly) employees required to work on an observed holiday or during Winter Recess receive 1.5x their regular hourly rate for hours worked on that day, in addition to their standard holiday pay for the day. Exempt (salaried) employees required to work on a holiday do not receive additional pay, since their compensation is not calculated on an hourly basis, but may take an alternate day off within 30 days with manager approval to preserve the intent of the holiday.

## Holidays Falling on a Weekend

When a fixed holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. When a fixed holiday falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday. The People & Culture Team publishes the exact observed dates for the upcoming calendar year in Compass HR no later than November 1 of the prior year, so employees can plan travel and personal PTO around the full holiday calendar well in advance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

I am on an approved leave of absence when a holiday occurs - do I still get holiday pay? Yes, holidays occurring during an approved paid leave (such as parental leave) are paid as a holiday rather than drawing down the leave balance, consistent with the Leave of Absence Policy (POL-LEV-001).

Can I use a floating holiday and a fixed holiday on the same day? No, floating holidays are for additional days beyond the fixed list in Section 2 and cannot be stacked with a fixed holiday that already falls on that date.

What if I am hired right as Winter Recess begins? New hires starting during Winter Recess have their start date and pay begin as normal; Winter Recess is a paid closure for active employees, and the People & Culture Team will confirm the exact arrangement with the new hire directly.

## Roles and Responsibilities

The People & Culture Team owns the annual holiday calendar, publishes observed dates each November, and confirms local public holiday schedules for EOR-employed international staff each year. Managers are responsible for planning around Winter Recess and holiday coverage needs in customer-facing functions well in advance, and for approving any alternate-day requests from exempt employees who worked a holiday.

Payroll is responsible for applying correct holiday premium pay to non-exempt employees who work on an observed holiday or during Winter Recess, and for ensuring floating holiday usage is tracked separately from standard PTO in each pay period.
