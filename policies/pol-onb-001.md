# New Hire Onboarding Policy

**Document ID:** POL-ONB-001  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Version:** 1.3  
**Owner:** People & Culture Team

Describes the pre-start checklist, first-day/first-week milestones, the 30/60/90-day plan, and onboarding buddy program.

## Purpose

This policy describes the standard onboarding process for all new Solara Dynamics employees, ensuring every new hire has the access, equipment, and support needed to be productive quickly and to feel welcomed into the team from their very first day.

## Pre-Start Checklist

At least 5 business days before a new hire's start date, the People & Culture Team completes account provisioning in Compass HR, and IT ships equipment per the Company Equipment & Asset Policy (POL-EQP-001). Managers are notified to prepare a 30/60/90 day plan and assign an onboarding buddy before the start date, and are reminded to schedule introductory meetings with key collaborators during the new hire's first two weeks.

## First Day Schedule

New hires complete account activation, review key policies including this document and the Workplace Conduct & Anti-Harassment Policy (POL-CON-001), and meet with their manager and onboarding buddy. Remote and hybrid new hires join a virtual IT setup call to confirm VPN and equipment access, while onsite new hires complete a short office orientation with a Facilities team member.

## First Week Milestones

During the first week, new hires complete required compliance training (see Section 7), meet key cross-functional partners, and receive an overview of team goals and current projects from their manager. New hires are also enrolled in the next available new-hire orientation session hosted by the People & Culture Team, which covers company history, values, and an overview of available benefits.

## 30/60/90-Day Plan

Every new hire's manager documents role-specific goals for the first 30, 60, and 90 days in Compass HR. At each milestone, the manager and new hire hold a check-in to review progress, gather feedback on the onboarding experience, and adjust goals as needed for the next phase.

## Onboarding Buddy Program

Each new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy: a peer outside their direct reporting line who helps answer day-to-day questions during the first 90 days, from 'who do I ask about expense reports' to informal advice about team norms. Onboarding buddies are recognized in the quarterly People & Culture Team newsletter as a thank-you for their role in a new hire's success.

## Required Compliance Training

All new hires must complete Security Awareness Training, Anti-Harassment Training, and Code of Conduct Training within 14 calendar days of their start date. Managers are notified in Compass HR if training is not completed on time, and repeated reminders escalate to the People & Culture Team after 21 days.

## Equipment and Access Provisioning

Standard equipment and system access are provisioned according to role as defined in the Company Equipment & Asset Policy (POL-EQP-001). Access to Confidential or Restricted data systems requires manager approval in addition to standard provisioning, consistent with the Information Security & Data Protection Policy (POL-SEC-001).

## Onboarding for Remote New Hires

Remote new hires receive equipment shipped to their home address at least 3 business days before their start date and are invited to a live virtual welcome session with the People & Culture Team in addition to the standard first-day schedule, to help offset the reduced in-person interaction of a fully remote start.

## Frequently Asked Questions

My equipment did not arrive before my start date - what should I do? Contact the Solara IT Helpdesk immediately; your manager and onboarding buddy can help you get temporary access to web-based tools while a replacement shipment is arranged.

When do I get access to Confidential systems? Standard access is provisioned before your start date, but access to systems holding Confidential or Restricted data requires a specific manager approval, which is typically requested during your first week once your exact responsibilities are confirmed.

Do I need to complete onboarding training before I can use company systems? You can use standard systems from day one, but you must complete Security Awareness, Anti-Harassment, and Code of Conduct training within 14 days as described in Section 7.

## Roles and Responsibilities

The People & Culture Team owns the onboarding process end to end, provisions Compass HR accounts, and hosts new-hire orientation. The hiring manager is responsible for the 30/60/90 day plan, assigning an onboarding buddy, and providing day-to-day guidance during the new hire's first weeks.

IT is responsible for shipping and configuring equipment before the start date and for running the first-day setup call for remote and hybrid new hires. The new hire is responsible for completing required training on time and raising questions early rather than waiting until a 30/60/90 check-in.
