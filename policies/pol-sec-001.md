# Information Security & Data Protection Policy

**Document ID:** POL-SEC-001  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-15  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Owner:** Security & IT Team

Defines data classification levels, acceptable use, MFA/VPN requirements, BYOD rules, and incident reporting obligations.

## Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes minimum information security requirements for all Solara Dynamics employees, contractors, and interns who access company systems, applications, or data, regardless of work location or work arrangement. It applies equally to employees working from a company office, from home, or temporarily from another approved location under the Remote Work Policy (POL-RWK-001) or the Work Location, Interstate & International Work Policy (POL-LOC-001).

## Data Classification

All company and customer data falls into one of four classification levels, and every employee is responsible for handling data according to its classification regardless of which system it lives in:

- Public: approved for external release (marketing materials, published documentation, public job postings).
- Internal: general business information not intended for external release (internal wikis, meeting notes, non-sensitive internal announcements).
- Confidential: sensitive business information (financial forecasts, unreleased product plans, employee compensation and performance records).
- Restricted: customer production data, authentication credentials, and personally identifiable information (PII), subject to the strictest handling controls described throughout this policy.

## Acceptable Use of Company Systems

Company-issued accounts and equipment are provided for business use. Limited incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with job performance, violate law, or expose company systems to risk, for example installing unapproved software or browser extensions with broad data access, or using a company account to sign up for unrelated personal services.

Employees should have no expectation of privacy in their use of company systems, which may be monitored and reviewed consistent with applicable law and Solara Dynamics' internal monitoring standards, primarily for security and operational purposes rather than routine surveillance of individual employees.

## Password and Multi-Factor Authentication Requirements

All company accounts must use a unique password of at least 14 characters managed through the company password manager, and multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required on all systems that support it, with no exceptions for convenience. Shared logins are prohibited except for designated service accounts approved by IT Security and documented in the service account registry.

Employees who lose access to their MFA device should contact the Solara IT Helpdesk immediately for identity verification and account recovery rather than asking a colleague to share credentials or bypass MFA in any way.

## Use of Personal Devices (BYOD)

Personal devices may be used to access company email and chat applications after enrolling in the company's mobile device management (MDM) profile, which allows IT Security to enforce a device passcode and remotely wipe company data if the device is lost or the employee separates from the company. Personal devices may not be used to access, store, or process Confidential or Restricted data. All development, customer support, and data-handling work must be performed on company-issued equipment.

## Public Wi-Fi and VPN Requirements

Employees working from any location other than a Solara Dynamics office, including while traveling domestically or internationally under an approved Work Location Change Request, must connect to internal systems exclusively through SolaraSecure VPN. Use of public or unsecured Wi-Fi networks without VPN protection to access company systems is prohibited, whether from a coffee shop, airport, hotel, or coworking space.

SolaraSecure VPN is available for all company-issued laptops and should be connected before opening any internal application, email client, or customer data system. Employees experiencing persistent VPN connectivity issues while traveling should contact the Solara IT Helpdesk rather than working around the VPN requirement.

## Customer Data Handling

Restricted-classification customer data may only be accessed using company-issued equipment, over SolaraSecure VPN, from a location that complies with the Work Location, Interstate & International Work Policy (POL-LOC-001). Restricted data may not be exported to personal storage, personal email, or unapproved third-party services under any circumstances, including for the purpose of working around a technical limitation.

## Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident, including lost or stolen equipment, phishing attempts, or unauthorized account access, must be reported to security@solaradynamics.example or the Solara IT Helpdesk within 24 hours of discovery. Prompt reporting is not subject to disciplinary action, even if the employee's own action contributed to the incident, because early reporting significantly reduces the potential impact of most security incidents.

## International Data Transfer Considerations

Because data protection and export-control obligations vary by country, Restricted-classification data may only be accessed from the United States or from a country on the approved Employer of Record list in the Work Location, Interstate & International Work Policy (POL-LOC-001): United Kingdom, Canada, Portugal, and Ireland. Employees approved for international remote work under POL-LOC-001 must complete an additional Data Handling Acknowledgment with IT Security prior to departure if their role involves Restricted data, confirming they understand the additional handling restrictions that apply while traveling.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Can I check work email on my personal phone? Yes, after enrolling your device in the company's MDM profile as described in Section 5, you may use a personal phone for email and chat, but not for accessing Confidential or Restricted systems.

Is it safe to work from hotel Wi-Fi while traveling? Only if you connect through SolaraSecure VPN first; hotel, airport, and coffee shop networks are all considered public and unsecured under this policy regardless of whether they require a password.

What should I do if I accidentally click a phishing link? Report it immediately to security@solaradynamics.example or the Solara IT Helpdesk. Prompt reporting is never subject to disciplinary action, and quick action significantly reduces potential harm.

## Roles and Responsibilities

The Security & IT Team owns this policy, maintains the data classification standard, administers SolaraSecure VPN and MDM enrollment, and leads incident response for reported security events. Every employee is responsible for following the acceptable use, password, MFA, and VPN requirements in this policy and for reporting suspected incidents within 24 hours.

Managers are responsible for ensuring their direct reports complete Security Awareness Training on schedule and for promptly notifying IT Security if a departing employee's access needs to be revoked ahead of the standard offboarding process.
